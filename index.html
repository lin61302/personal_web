Between the lines.

01 / MediaOngoing research

Beyond tone

I study media bias by separating who acts, what they do, the implications for government reputation, and how a story frames it. The question: how do these choices relate to polarization?

Actor–action sentiment · reputation · framing
02 / GeographyManuscript in preparation

Putting news in its place

AI-assisted geoparsing connects place mentions in translated news to consistent provinces and districts. I’m developing a reconciliation layer that makes those locations usable for subnational research.

Hello, I’m Zung-Ru.

I’m a Taiwan-born researcher and data scientist at Penn’s PDRI-DevLab. I work where language, politics, and computation meet.

I like turning a difficult research question into something people can examine and use. Away from work, I follow food, dance, and the small details of a new place.
